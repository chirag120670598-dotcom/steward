What it can and cannot decide

Decides alone

  • whether tests still pass
  • whether a token is committed
  • whether a readme, licence or workflow is missing
  • whether anything is stale, oversized or junk

Needs a person

  • whether the code is any good
  • whether the product is worth building
  • anything that spends money or deploys
  • what to do about a failure it reported
